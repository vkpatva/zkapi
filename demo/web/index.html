Search without being tracked

Each search is paid from a private note with a proof made in your browser. The operator is paid, but can't tell who searched, or which searches came from the same person.

    Withdraw a note

    Take a note's remaining balance out of the vault, to any address. The operator receives what the note spent.

    Mutual close

    The normal exit. The operator co-signs your final balance and one transaction pays you. No waiting.

    Escape

    Exit without the operator, if it is offline or refuses. Paid after the challenge period, during which the operator can contest a stale state. Finalize it from the Notes tab.
